BETWEEN THE SCREEN & THE SKY

Another kind
of field work.

A place to collect the walks that would otherwise slip away. Each line is a route actually walked; each card, a small record of time spent outside.

Made with a developer’s curiosity and a walker’s pace.

About the routes & numbers +

Distance is estimated from the original GPS track. Elapsed time runs from the first to the last recorded point and includes stops. Average pace is elapsed time divided by distance. These estimates may differ from Apple Fitness.

Every recorded GPS point is retained, including the start and finish. The replay draws for seven seconds, holds the whole route for three seconds, then begins again. It follows the route geometry rather than real-time walking speed.

Route coordinates, dates, timing and altitude are retained. GPX metadata, track names, original filenames, device information and extension fields are excluded. No other health records are included.

“Areas explored” counts approximate proximity groups of route midpoints within 25 km, not named districts or land area. Summary statistics refer to the full original walks, and the maps show the complete tracks. Card titles are editorial labels.
